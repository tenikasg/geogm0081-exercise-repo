My favourite place in (near) Bristol is Tyntesfield. I used to go a lot as a child with my grandparents, and now I force all my uni friends to go with me! It's a National Trust place; they do a great pumpkin display every Autumn and have actors at Christmas who pretend to be the Victorian Residents (also great mulled wine). It is spenny, but you can bring your own mulled wine tbf.  Also near Clevedon, which is lovely if you haven't been - great Christmas parkrun.}
